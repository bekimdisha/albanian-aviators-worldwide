What we do

Built by pilots, for pilots.

Whatever stage of your aviation career you're at, the network is here to help you connect, learn, and grow.

Mentorship

Experienced captains and first officers guide the next generation of Albanian aviators through every stage of licensing and career growth.

Career Opportunities

Members share airline openings, cadet programs, and referrals across our network of carriers and flight schools worldwide.

Global Community

From Tirana to Toronto, meet fellow pilots at regional meetups, annual gatherings, and online events throughout the year.
