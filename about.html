About Us

Our mission is the sky we share.

Albanian Aviators Worldwide exists to connect, support, and celebrate pilots of Albanian heritage — wherever their careers have taken them.

Our Story

From a small group chat to a global network.

Albanian Aviators Worldwide began in 2019 when a handful of commercial pilots scattered across Europe and North America started a group chat to trade notes on type ratings and airline hiring. Word spread fast — within a year, cadets, flight instructors, and military aviators of Albanian descent from more than a dozen countries had joined.

Today we're a structured community with a mentorship program, regional chapters, and an annual in-person gathering, but the spirit hasn't changed: pilots helping pilots, no matter which flag is on the tail.

Our Mission

To connect Albanian pilots and aviation professionals worldwide through mentorship, shared opportunity, and community — strengthening the presence of Albanian aviators in the global industry.

What We Believe

Our values

Mentorship over gatekeeping

Every member who's found their seat has a responsibility to help the next person find theirs.

Community across borders

Our members fly for dozens of airlines and armed forces around the world — the network comes first.

Professional excellence

We hold each other to the highest standard, on the flight deck and in how we represent the community.

Open to all backgrounds

Commercial, military, general aviation, cadets, and aviation professionals — all are welcome here.

Milestones

How we've grown

2019

Founded as an informal group

A small circle of pilots begins sharing resources and referrals online.

2021

First regional chapters formed

Members in Germany, the UK, and the US establish local chapters and meetups.

2023

Mentorship program launched

A formal pairing program connects cadets and student pilots with active captains.

2025

First annual gathering held in Tirana

Members from over a dozen countries reunite in person for the first time.
